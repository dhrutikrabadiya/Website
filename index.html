Our Core Products & Solutions

PCC Panels (Power Control Centers)

Designed for high-capacity electrical power distribution. Ideal for heavy industrial plants, utility substations, and infrastructure projects. Built to ensure safety, scalability, and performance.

MCC Panels (Motor Control Centers)

Centralized motor control systems used in industrial automation, offering fault protection, easy maintenance, and energy efficiency through advanced motor control units.

APFC Panels (Automatic Power Factor Correction)

Microcontroller-based panels that automatically regulate power factor, reduce penalty charges, and optimize energy usage for commercial and industrial facilities.

Control Panels (PLC / Automation)

Custom-designed panels for automation, machinery, and process control using PLC, SCADA, and HMI interfaces. Engineered for reliability and smart factory operations.

Fire Pump Controller Panels

NFPA-compliant panels that ensure reliable startup and monitoring of fire pumps in critical safety systems. Suitable for high-rise buildings, hospitals, and industrial safety zones.

Distribution Boards & Bus Duct Systems

Tailor-made low-voltage distribution boards and aluminum/copper bus ducts for efficient power transfer across floors and machines in large facilities.
